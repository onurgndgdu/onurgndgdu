Two honest notes. Shadwell claimed to be the first to diagnose bicycle
face, but the phrase was already in print by 1895 — the video says
"claimed" for that reason. And the Susan B. Anthony line is widely
repeated but I could not verify it against the original Nellie Bly
interview, so it is described rather than quoted.
