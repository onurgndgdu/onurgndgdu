=== AÇIKLAMA ===
February 1959. Searchers found the tent on Kholat Syakhl in the northern Urals, slashed open from the inside. Nine experienced ski hikers had fled into the dark in temperatures around -30°C, some in socks, some barefoot. Their footprints led down the slope toward the treeline. None of them survived.

For more than sixty years it was one of the most argued-about mysteries of the Soviet era. In 2021, researchers Johan Gaume (EPFL) and Alexander Puzrin (ETH Zurich) published a model in Communications Earth & Environment showing that a small, delayed slab avalanche could explain the cut tent and many of the injuries.

The Mansi name of the mountain, Kholat Syakhl, is usually translated as "Dead Mountain."
